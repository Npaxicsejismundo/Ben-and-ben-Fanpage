## Story (verified)
- 2016: twins Paolo & Miguel Benjamin Guico (graduated De La Salle 2016) form "The Benjamins" (shared second name). PhilPop 2016 entry "Tinatangi": 3rd place (2nd runner-up) + Best Music Video. Renamed Ben&Ben; self-titled EP Dec 17, 2016 (Sindikato). Sources: Wikipedia (cites Rappler, CNN PH, MB); Manila Bulletin 2022-03-23 (archived https://web.archive.org/web/20240120222228/https://uplift.mb.com.ph/benben-uplift-arts-culture-award/); Rappler 2021-08-08.
- May 10, 2017: officially a nine-piece. Bandwagon 2021-05-07 https://bandwagon.asia/articles/ben-and-ben-comeback-new-era-upuan-song-single-music-lyric-video-watch-2021
- First song as nine-piece: "Maybe the Night", arranged in Jam's attic in 2017. Bandwagon 2026-09-24 https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/ben-ben-look-back-on-9-years-together-with-anthology-album-nine-listen
- Fandom named "Liwanag" on 2020-09-04 (previous "LIGHTs" clashed with HIGHLIGHT's fandom). Bandwagon https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/ben-and-ben-new-name-fandom-liwanag-highlight-lights-fans-2020
- Jam Villanueva: announced 2025-12-11 (video on band socials) that 2026 is her last year. GMA 2025-12-12 https://www.gmanetwork.com/entertainment/showbiznews/benbens-jam-villanueva-set-to-leave-the-band-after-2026/128635/ ; Billboard PH 2025-12-12 https://billboardphilippines.com/music/news/jam-villanueva-announces-departure-from-ben-and-ben-2026-statement/ . Reasons (paraphrase): 2024 marriage + April 2025 wrist injury (De Quervain's, per DZRH/BusinessWorld) reshaped her priorities. She revealed she is the "saranggola" of the song.
- Final nine-piece show: Saranggola: The Concert, Smart Araneta Coliseum, 2026-10-02. DZRH 2026-10-03 https://www.dzrh.com.ph/post/benandbens-jam-villanueva-takes-final-stage-after-nine-years-with-the-band ; Inquirer 2026-10-04 https://entertainment.inquirer.net/685688/benben-marks-9-years-closes-nine-piece-chapter-with-saranggola-concert ; PEP 2026-10-04 https://www.pep.ph/lifestyle/celebrations/194105/saranggola-ben-ben-nine-years-a6904-20261004-lfrm ; BusinessWorld 2026-10-05.

## Members (roles verified; facts paraphrased, cite source)
Sources: BW19 = Bandwagon Doodles 2019-05-08 https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/bandwagon-doodles-ben-ben-members-tell-their-stories-instruments-and-interests-interview-2019 ; ESQ = Esquire PH 2022-01-17 https://www.esquiremag.ph/culture/music/ben-and-ben-esquire-sessions-live-podcast-a00304-20220117 ; NME = NME 2021-08 https://www.nme.com/features/benben-album-pebble-house-vol-1-kuwaderno-kz-tandingan-sb19-zild-3032643 ; PEP25 = PEP 2025-05-19 https://www.pep.ph/news/local/ben-and-ben-on-misunderstandings-visiting-cancer-patients-a716-20250519-lfrm ; BW26 = Bandwagon 2026-09-24 (Nine article above)

- Paolo Benjamin (vocals, acoustic guitar): guitar and singing "chose" him; he plays to get songs out (BW19). Relates to underdogs; GoT pick Brienne (BW19). First concert: Urbandub at Alabang Town Center (ESQ). Idea for every member to produce songs on Pebble House (NME, per Poch).
- Miguel Benjamin (vocals, acoustic guitar): twins' high-school band "Deadline"; dad made shirts in Comic Sans; got acoustic guitars after passing Engineering (BW19). Learned video editing in a college org; makes band posters, sometimes 2AM (BW19). Usually mediates band arguments (PEP25).
- Poch Barretto (lead guitar): football first love (BW19). Guitarist mom taught him at 9; met Miguel at songwriting camp; played for Clara Benin (BW19). Watches + Rubik's cubes (BW19). Fave Beatle George Harrison (ESQ). Led full production of Nine (BW26).
- Keifer Cabugao (violin): wanted guitar, mom "tricked" him into violin; a tito told him to stick with it (BW19). Fave album Bon Iver self-titled (ESQ). Band isn't perfect, fights, but love runs deeper (PEP25).
- Pat Lasaten (keyboards): musician dad pushed piano; loved it by high school (BW19). Self-described organizer, loves timelines; longboarding (BW19). First song learned: a Hannah Montana tune (ESQ). Married bandmate Agnes (ABS-CBN 2024-11-18 https://abs-cbn.com/entertainment/2024/11/18/ben-ben-s-pat-and-agnes-are-now-married-1319).
- Agnes Reoma (bass): started on guitar, pretended she could play bass in college, kept being recruited (BW19). Fave film Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (ESQ). Led "Lunod" on Kuwaderno (NME). "Paninindigan Kita" video (2022-06-30) tells Pat & Agnes's story (Bandwagon 2022-07-01 https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/ben-ben-pat-lasaten-and-agnes-reoma-relive-their-love-story-in-new-music-video-for-paninindigan-kita-sony-music-philippines-june-2022).
- Toni Muñoz (percussion, vocals): dodged childhood piano by claiming dizziness (BW19). UP composition major; percussion minor = found her voice (BW19). Always carries a camera, even onstage (BW19). "Sugat" (co-written w/ Paolo) began as a love letter to herself (GMA 2021-08-13 https://gmanetwork.com/news/lifestyle/hobbiesandactivities/799193/ben-amp-ben-drops-music-video-for-sugat-featuring-munimuni/story).
- Andrew de Pano (percussion): told the twins he could play percussion before he could, then bought an instrument; still learning on the job (BW19). Cooks, theater, gaming, mountain biking, restored guitars (BW19). His airport-delay Switch led the band to buy one (BW19, per Agnes). Arranged/produced "Pasalubong" (Bandwagon 2021-07-30). Fave book The Giver (ESQ).
- Jam Villanueva (drums, 2017–2026): parents say drums at 4, she remembers 7; first kit in Grade 3 as reward for perfect computer exam (BW19). Film = "second love", Hot Fuzz got her into it (BW19). "Maybe the Night" arranged in her attic (BW26). The "saranggola" of the song (Billboard PH 2025-12-12).
- Only verified verbatim quote (Jam, GMA/Billboard PH): "Next year, 2026, will be my last year with the band."
